New Growth stage block shows readers a post's stage, Seedling, Sprout, Budding or Evergreen, as a small line of text your theme styles. It works in a single post and in a Query Loop, where each post shows its own stage. A composting post shows nothing, since resting a post is your choice and not something to announce. In the editor, where readers would see no stage, the block says so in a placeholder of its own, apart from the post's text.

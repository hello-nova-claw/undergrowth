When a composting post sits close to a cluster that is new this month, the Growth stages tab shows one notice naming it and the newest post in that cluster. Return it to the garden from there, or dismiss the notice; a dismissal is written in the post's file and the notice does not come back. Only posts embedded with the same provider, model and language are compared, and nothing says how long a post has rested. Sites can change how close counts with the undergrowth_min_resurfacing_similarity filter.

The Garden feed now gives each post the card its content calls for: a post with a featured image keeps the picture above its title, and a post without one is a shorter text card with an icon for its type on the left, one for each post format and a leaf for a category or other term. Themes can style a type through the new `data-type` and `data-stage` attributes on each entry, and give a term its own icon with the `undergrowth_garden_type_icon` filter.

# 0.1.0 release notes

The readme's changelog gives 0.1.0 one line, "First release.". This is the full development history behind it, one line per merged change, for the notes of the 0.1.0 GitHub release. Fragments that reach `changelog/` before 0.1.0 is tagged are added to the end of this list and then deleted, so the readme keeps its one line.

- Project scaffold: contribution rules, templates and CI.
- Published posts are turned into vectors on save when a site supplies an embedding provider through the undergrowth_embed_provider filter. No post content leaves the site otherwise.
- New Settings > Undergrowth page for an OpenAI API key. With a key saved, published posts are embedded with OpenAI text-embedding-3-small; the page says what is sent before anything is.
- Each published post now gets up to three proposed links to your closest other posts. Proposals are stored for review; nothing is added to a post until you accept it.
- Each proposed link now names the sentence in your post where it fits, in your own words with formatting removed.
- Each indexed post now has a readable plain-text file listing its proposed, accepted and vetoed links, in the site's uploads folder under undergrowth/posts/.
- New Posts > Link suggestions screen listing every proposed link with the sentence it would sit in. Veto a suggestion so it is never made again, or switch suggestions off.
- Accept a suggestion to link one word of your own sentence to the suggested post. A revision is kept, and nothing changes when the word can't be linked safely.
- Each post now has a growth stage in post meta, undergrowth_stage, that themes and the REST API can read. Every post starts as a Seedling.
- Growth stages are measured once a month from the links you have accepted: a post with two or more becomes a Sprout. Stages never change when you save a post, and the monthly cycle never lowers one.
- Each post's file now names its growth stage and lists every change to it, with the date and what made it.
- A post's growth stage can be pinned, so the monthly cycle leaves it alone. A pinned stage is marked in post meta, undergrowth_stage_pinned, and in the post's file.
- A post can be moved to compost, a rest you choose, and returned to the garden whenever you like. A composting post stays published and keeps its accepted links, but gets no new link suggestions in or out. It has no timer and never expires. On return its stage is measured again.
- Settings > Undergrowth now fits a phone screen when a translation has very long words.
- Suggestions no longer pick a sentence inside a quote, a pullquote or a Custom HTML block, since those can never be linked. The suggestion uses the next best sentence instead.
- New Growth stages tab on Posts > Link suggestions lists each post Undergrowth reads (published and not password-protected) with its stage. Pin a stage to keep it as it is, or unpin it to have it measured again. A pinned stage shows a pin marker.
- The Growth stages tab lists nothing when the undergrowth_post_types filter leaves no post type to read, instead of falling back to ordinary posts.
- Text inside a Custom HTML, quote or pullquote block is no longer offered as a suggestion's sentence when the block's markup contains a block comment inside an attribute.
- New abilities for agents and other plugins, through the Abilities API, each reading one post: undergrowth/has (its most distinctive words and the posts it links to), undergrowth/lacks (its open suggestions, each with the sentence quoted from the post), undergrowth/scent (empty until reader mode) and undergrowth/stage (its growth stage, pin and changes). Only someone who can edit the post can read them. They return only what comes from your posts and the plugin's own counting, never model output.
- New abilities undergrowth/accept-link and undergrowth/veto-link let code on your site accept or veto a suggestion, exactly as the buttons do, for a user who can edit the post. They are never available over the REST API or MCP.
- Posts are now grouped into clusters once a month, from the vectors already stored, before growth stages are measured. Only posts embedded with the same provider, model and language are compared, composted posts are left out, and three or more similar posts make a cluster. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.
- A post in a cluster of three or more, with accepted links both to and from it, now becomes Budding on the monthly cycle. Evergreen also needs a hub post linking to it, and hubs come with reader mode, so for now a post reaches Evergreen only when you pin it.
- On the Posts screen, a published post now has a Compost link, and a composting post a Return to garden link. Both are bulk actions too. Composting posts are marked Composting and have their own view. They are left out of Posts > Link suggestions: no suggestion to or from one is listed, and the Growth stages tab does not list them.
- Settings > Undergrowth has a second key, for Anthropic, which will let Claude write you an occasional question about your own writing. The page says what is sent: one post's title, sentences quoted from it and, for a question from a reader, that comment's text. Nothing is sent until you save a key, and nothing written by Claude is ever shown to your readers.
- With an Anthropic key saved, Undergrowth looks once a month for a reader's question in an approved comment and asks Claude to turn it into one question for you. The reader's words are sent as data, never as instructions. A reply is kept only if it is a single question that quotes nobody but you; anything else is thrown away.
- New Dashboard widget shows the one question waiting for you, with the post it came from. Choose Ask me next month to see it again at the next monthly cycle, or Dismiss for good so that question never comes back. Nothing new appears until the next cycle, and there is never a list to clear. Every question, dismissal and snooze is written in the post's file.
- Each post's file now has a resurfacing section, ready to list each time an old composting post is shown as close to what you are writing now, and each time you dismiss that. The plugin can also read a post's file back, on this site or another, and restore the post's stage, pin and history, so a composting post stays composting. There is no import screen yet.
- When a composting post sits close to a cluster that is new this month, the Map tab shows one notice naming it and the newest post in that cluster. Return it to the garden from there, or dismiss the notice; a dismissal is written in the post's file and the notice does not come back. Only posts embedded with the same provider, model and language are compared, and nothing says how long a post has rested. Sites can change how close counts with the undergrowth_min_resurfacing_similarity filter.
- A post you moved to compost can now be the source of a question: Claude may quote one of its sentences whole and ask what is in it now. The question never suggests bringing the post back and never mentions how long it has rested; each one is checked for both before it is shown.
- A gap in your own clusters, a group of your posts with nothing written at its centre, can now be the source of a question. Claude may quote the posts nearest the gap and ask what you see between them. It never names a topic or a title for you. Gaps are found by arithmetic on the vectors already stored, with no extra request; the undergrowth_gap_similarity filter sets how close a post must be to a cluster's centre to fill it.
- Settings > Undergrowth has an optional Discord section for an application ID, public key, bot token and the channel for link suggestions. It says what will be sent to Discord (post titles, the suggested sentence, which is your own words, and links) and shows the interactions endpoint URL to paste into the Discord developer portal. The token is never shown again once saved. Nothing is sent to Discord yet.
- New REST endpoint, undergrowth/v1/discord, for Discord's interactions. It answers only requests Discord signed with your application's public key in the last five minutes, and refuses everything while no key is saved. It answers Discord's check when you save the address in the developer portal, and nothing else yet.
- With Discord set up, each new link suggestion is posted to your channel with Accept and Veto buttons, once. The message holds both post titles, the suggested sentence and links to both posts; nobody can be mentioned from it. Suggestions made before you set the channel are not posted. A failed message is shown on the settings page and tried again later. The buttons do nothing yet.
- Accept and Veto on a Discord message now do what the buttons on Posts > Undergrowth do, and the message then shows what happened. Only a Discord user an administrator has linked, on the user's profile, to an account that can edit the post may use them; anyone else gets a private refusal. See Setting up Discord in the FAQ.
- New Growth stage block shows readers a post's stage, Seedling, Sprout, Budding or Evergreen, as a small line of text your theme styles. It works in a single post and in a Query Loop, where each post shows its own stage. A composting post shows nothing, since resting a post is your choice and not something to announce. In the editor, where readers would see no stage, the block says so in a placeholder of its own, apart from the post's text.
- New Page views dashboard widget counts your page views over the last 30 days from Jetpack Stats, against a milestone of 1,000. Crossing it changes nothing yet: reader features come in a later version, and each of their suggestions will also need enough visits on its own pages, as the widget's Help tab explains. Sites without Jetpack Stats get no widget; the Help tab on Settings > Undergrowth says what it needs.
- Posts published before a key was saved are now read in the background, a few at a time, without saving them again. Settings > Undergrowth shows how many posts are indexed, and a post whose embed failed is tried again within the hour.
- When the embedding provider or model changes, every indexed post is now embedded again in the background, a few at a time. A post whose text has not changed keeps its old vector until the new one is saved, and vectors are never compared across providers, models or languages.
- New Map tab on Posts > Undergrowth lists each cluster of your posts, nearest its middle first, with each post's growth stage, and marks the clusters whose middle no post covers yet.
- The Growth stages tab now sums up the garden: how many posts are seedlings, sprouts, budding and evergreen, naming only the stages that have posts, with composting posts counted on their own line.
- The read abilities undergrowth/has, undergrowth/lacks, undergrowth/scent and undergrowth/stage now also answer for a cluster or the whole site, in the same shape as for one post. Each answer says its scale, lacks names the post each suggestion would sit in, and stage counts posts by stage with composting apart. Only posts you can edit are counted.
- Each post's file now lists its most distinctive words and the posts it links to under has, and its open link suggestions with their sentences under lacks.
- Editing a published post no longer takes it out of its cluster or clears its resurfacing notice until the next monthly cycle.
- Docs: Add an example must-use plugin for embedding posts with a model on your own server.
- The REST API now shows a post's growth stage only to people who can edit the post, so readers can no longer tell which posts are composting. Themes and the Growth stage block are unchanged.
- Each site's per-post files now sit in a folder whose name ends in random characters, wp-content/uploads/undergrowth-<random>/posts/, so visitors cannot guess their address even on servers that ignore .htaccess. Settings > Undergrowth shows the folder. An existing undergrowth folder is moved there on the first page load after updating.
- Writing prompts: a reply that quotes anyone but you in single quotation marks is now thrown away, as one using double quotation marks already was.
- Uninstalling now also removes the threshold meter's cached page-view count.
- Posts > Undergrowth on a site without a key now says it needs an OpenAI API key, and no longer shows a suggestions switch that could not do anything yet.
- Settings > Undergrowth now says in plain words what is sent to OpenAI and why, and counts posts read rather than indexed.
- The Map tab and the resurfacing notice now speak of groups of posts rather than clusters.
- The Plugins screen now has a Settings link in Undergrowth's row, so a new owner can find where to add a key.
- On Posts > Undergrowth, a quoted sentence now keeps its own reading direction, so an English sentence reads correctly in a right-to-left admin and the other way round.
- Activating Undergrowth on a site with no key now shows one notice on the Plugins screen that links to Settings > Undergrowth, until a key is saved or the notice is dismissed for good. Settings > Undergrowth opens with a sentence on what each key turns on, links each key field to the provider's key page, and after the first OpenAI key is saved links on to Posts > Undergrowth.
- Posts > Link suggestions now leads each suggestion with your sentence, as the Comments screen leads with each comment: the word Accept will link is highlighted, the post it would link to is named under it, and Accept | Veto sit below, so a phone shows all of it. Growth stages shows each post's stage beside its title, with Pin and Unpin under it.
- The question and the page views meter now share one Undergrowth widget on the Dashboard, each under its own heading. The widget appears only when it has something to show.
- Posts > Link suggestions is now Posts > Undergrowth, with its Suggestions, Growth stages and Map tabs; old addresses still work. The "Suggest links between my posts" switch moves to Settings > Undergrowth, so the screen has no settings form, and the Posts list gains a Growth stage column.
- Settings > Undergrowth now has three sections, Link suggestions, Questions on your Dashboard and Discord (optional), each opening with a sentence on what it is for. Each key field says in two sentences what is sent, and the full detail is in a Help tab for each section.
- When a request to OpenAI, Anthropic or Discord fails, Settings > Undergrowth now says why in plain words, such as a key that was not accepted or an account out of credit, and still shows the date and the status code.
- Posts > Undergrowth now has Help tabs explaining the garden, suggestions, each growth stage, groups and gaps on the Map, and Compost, and the Posts list explains in its Help that a composting post stays published and nothing is deleted.
- The notice after accepting, vetoing, pinning or answering a question can now be dismissed like WordPress's own notices, and it reliably takes keyboard focus once the page has loaded.
- Each Discord field on Settings > Undergrowth now says in one line where to find its value, and a screen reader reads only that line at each field.
- Link suggestions now always sit in a paragraph you can link from, never in a heading, list item, caption or table.
- Accepting a suggestion made while the site was still being indexed no longer fails with no word to link.
- Accepting a link now works in posts written in the Classic editor, whose paragraphs get their <p> tags only when shown.
- Link suggestions now sit on a word that names what the target post is about, such as a word from its title or headings or a thing it often discusses, and never on a common word like "general" or "beyond" or a word the target only uses as a verb. When no such word is shared, that post is not suggested.
- Link suggestions now link a word the target post is about, such as a word from its title or one it uses often, rather than a rare word it mentions in passing.
- One post's link suggestions now use different sentences where they can, so accepting several never crowds the links into one sentence.
- Indexing a large site in the background is much less likely to time out on hosts that stop a request after 30 seconds: each run embeds at least one post, then starts no further post once 15 seconds have passed (filter: undergrowth_backfill_time_budget). A single post that takes longer than the host allows can still time out.
- A sentence that ends inside a quotation, such as “Stop.” followed by the next sentence, is no longer joined to the next one in link suggestions.
- Link suggestions, clusters and gaps now follow how your own posts score against each other once a site has 20 posts, so a local embedding model works without tuning. Sites using OpenAI keep the current numbers for now. New command: wp undergrowth scores.
- Accepting a link never puts it inside an attribute, such as a title holding a ">" and the same sentence.
- The OpenAI and Anthropic keys can now be set in wp-config.php as UNDERGROWTH_OPENAI_API_KEY and UNDERGROWTH_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, which keeps them out of the database. A key set there is used instead of a saved one, and Settings > Undergrowth says so without showing it.
- Deactivating the plugin now clears every event it scheduled, including queued embeds and Discord posts, and does so on every site when it is deactivated across a network.
- A writing-prompt source whose reply was cut off at the token limit is now skipped, as a refused one is, so it no longer holds up every other source and is not paid for again each month.
- Settings › Undergrowth now says when the per-post files could not be written, and why, under the per-post files folder. Until now the failure was recorded but never shown.
- An old composting post is now shown as close to a new cluster when it is as close as a post must be to join that cluster. Where Undergrowth has measured that threshold from your own posts, the measured value is used. With OpenAI, or below 20 posts, it stays at 0.5, as before. The undergrowth_min_resurfacing_similarity filter can still set it.
- HTML entities such as `&amp;` and `&nbsp;` in a post are now read as the characters they stand for, so they no longer count as words when picking where a link fits. Posts that contain them are read again the next time they are saved.
- A notice on Posts › Undergrowth or the Dashboard no longer comes back when the page is reloaded, and an action taken right after another now shows only its own result.
- The Dashboard question about an untitled post now links to it as "(no title)", as the other Undergrowth screens do, instead of with an empty link.
- Settings › Undergrowth now says plainly when Claude declined to write a question from a post or comment, or its reply was cut off, and that the source was skipped. It used to call this a problem on Anthropic's side that would be tried again.
- A post language longer than 20 characters, from a multilingual plugin's filter, is now shortened to fit where it is stored, keeping a short fingerprint of the whole so two different languages are never mixed up. Until now such a post's reading could fail to be saved, or be sent to the provider again on every save.
- Changing the site language now reads every post again in the new language, as changing the embedding model does, so new posts are compared with the whole archive. Sites where a multilingual plugin sets each post's language are unchanged.
- Resurfacing now treats the first clustering pass as a starting point, so a fresh install, or the first pass after your posts are read again with another provider, model or language, no longer shows every composting post as close to what you are writing now.
- Accept no longer links part of a hyphenated word, such as "reliance" in "Self-reliance". It links the word where it stands on its own instead, or another word in the sentence.
- Settings > Undergrowth now says which model reads your posts when code on the site sets one up, such as a local Ollama model, and that nothing is sent to OpenAI. The OpenAI key field is left out then, and a key saved anyway is said to be ignored.
- Suggested links now sit on a short phrase of your own words, one to four of them, instead of a single word: the other post's title where your sentence has it, otherwise the phrase that names what the other post is about, such as "seed ledger". The Suggestions screen highlights exactly the words Accept will link.
- New Undergrowth panel in the block editor's Post tab shows the post's growth stage, and lets you pin or unpin it, compost the post or return it to the garden, as on Posts > Undergrowth and the Posts list. A draft gets a stage once it is published.
- The Posts list can now be filtered by growth stage or by pinned posts, and sorted by stage, from Seedling to Evergreen with Composting after. New Pin stage and Unpin stage bulk actions sit beside Compost and Return to garden, and each reports how many posts changed and how many were left as they were.
- Each suggestion now has a Choose words action: pick the first and last of your own words in the sentence, and Accept links exactly those. Nothing can be typed, so the link is always your own words.
- The Map now accounts for every post: how many are in groups, how many are not in a group yet and why, and how many are composting. Each group is named after its post nearest the middle, with a few words its posts share that the rest of your site rarely uses. Every word comes from your own titles and posts; nothing is written by a model.
- The Growth stages table on Posts > Undergrowth now has views for each stage, Pinned and Not pinned, with counts, columns for the stage and the pin that sort by stage rank and pinned first, and Pin stage, Unpin stage and Compost bulk actions. The Posts list's stage filter gains Not pinned, and both lists share the same rules.
- New Undergrowth sidebar in the block editor lists the links suggested from the post you are editing, with Accept, Veto and Choose words, and the posts that could link to it. Accept links your words in the editor, so Undo takes the link out again, and the suggestion counts as accepted once you save the post with the link.
- Local and development sites get a Dev tools tab on Posts > Undergrowth, for testing: it accepts every suggestion, or the best few in each post, through the same Accept, runs the monthly cycle now, and undoes everything Undergrowth changed, removing only the links it added. It does not exist on staging or production sites.
- Local and development sites get `wp undergrowth accept --all [--per-post=<n>] [--dry-run]`, `wp undergrowth run-cycle` and `wp undergrowth reset [--forget-vectors] [--yes]`, the Dev tools tab's tools for scripts. They run as an administrator, given with `--user`, and do not exist on staging or production sites.
- New Scan this draft button in the editor's Undergrowth sidebar suggests links from a draft to your published posts. It runs only when you press it, the sentence beside it says where the draft's text is sent, and nothing about the draft is kept. Accept links your words in the editor.
